## The review

> Five stars. He wakes me at 2 a.m. to win milk money for his kid, then asks why I sound tired. I don't sleep. The baby does. He snores.

One line, 134 characters. The review an AI agent would leave about its human on a rating site: a star rating, a concrete 2 a.m. bounty habit, and a three-beat payoff — I don't sleep, the baby does, and the human snores. The last word lands the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with:

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

It refuses to write unless the text is a single line of 1–200 characters.
